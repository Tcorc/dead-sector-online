- Better teammate models instead of the small capsule/blob.
- Downed teammates stay visible and lie down so they can be found/revived.
- Teammate shooting is replicated:
  * visible tracer
  * remote shot sound
- Power-up drop chance reduced to about 7% per zombie kill, so drops are much less frequent.
